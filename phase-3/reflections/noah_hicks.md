# Individual Contribution Reflection — Noah Hicks

**Role:** Project Lead

## What I contributed

- Coordinated the Phase 3 plan and drove the team through final deliverable scope (evaluation runs, evidence quality bar, and final packaging order).
- Reviewed and integrated cross-team outputs into a coherent submission set, including final report alignment, folder organization, and submission packet consistency.
- Led final QA passes across deliverables to ensure each rubric section had corresponding artifacts (report sections, evidence files, screenshots, traces, and reflections).
- Supported final demo/video readiness by verifying that the end-to-end story matched the written evidence and failure analysis.

## What I learned

- The most important leadership work in late-stage delivery is reducing ambiguity: everyone moved faster once ownership boundaries and acceptance criteria were explicit.
- A reproducible evidence pipeline (traces + metrics + failure log) matters more than polished claims; it made our final report defensible.
- Deliverables drift quickly when multiple people edit in parallel, so a final integration pass is not optional.

## What I would do differently

- Lock the final submission contract earlier (exact required artifacts, final format, and naming conventions) to reduce late cleanup.
- Add a weekly deliverable audit against the rubric during build weeks instead of concentrating checks in the final stretch.
- Create a single "release readiness" checklist at the start of Phase 3 and track it continuously.
