Monitor existing customer accounts for churn-risk signals: drop in product usage over the last 30 days, support tickets tagged negative-sentiment, and late or failed invoice payments. Score each account's churn risk from 0 to 100. For scores above 75, immediately notify the assigned Customer Success Manager and draft a personalized retention outreach email. For scores between 40 and 75, add the account to a weekly CSM review queue. For scores below 40, continue passive monitoring. Log every scoring decision with the input signals for audit.
