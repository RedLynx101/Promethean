When a new sales opportunity is created in the CRM, verify that the deal has an assigned owner, a stated dollar value, and a target close date. Check the opportunity against historical win-rate data for similar deals and assign a deal-stage confidence score. For high-confidence deals above 50000 dollars, automatically schedule a proposal-prep meeting with the sales engineering team. For deals that have stalled more than 14 days without activity, flag the opportunity for sales manager review and draft a re-engagement email for the rep. Record every stage transition with timestamp and triggering event.
